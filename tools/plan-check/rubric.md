# Rubric: is this plan ready to post and build from?

## Checks

| Check | Evidence | Pass condition | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| diagnosis-grounded | Diagnosis and grounding: the plan's stated cause compared with the issue, repro inputs, actual output, controls, and relevant maintainer analysis. | The proposed cause explains the reproduced failure and its controls without contradicting them. An unconfirmed mechanism is acceptable when identified as a hypothesis with a concrete way to check it before implementing the dependent change. | required |
| cause-addressed | Diagnosis and grounding: the proposed edit traced from the failing operation in the repro through the plan's mechanism. | The change acts on the cause implicated by the evidence and can produce the expected behavior. Hiding an exception, suppressing output, or bypassing the failing feature does not pass unless that fallback is the intended behavior supported by the package. | required |
| bounded-scope | Scope: the plan's included behavior, exclusions, named areas, and each proposed edit compared with the issue's requested outcome. | Every proposed change is necessary for one bounded fix or its directly related tests and documentation; unrelated cleanup, redesign, new features, and speculative future-proofing are excluded. Judge dependencies and behavior, not the number of files. | required |
| executable-approach | Executability: the plan's edit locations, proposed behavior or algorithm, ordering, and resolution of prerequisites. | A contributor familiar with the repository can locate the relevant code and start the proposed change without inventing the central design. Concrete modules or functions can suffice without exact line numbers; "investigate and fix" alone cannot. | required |
| observable-regression | Test plan: the plan's tests compared with the repro's trigger, steps, observed failure, expected result, and controls. | At least one specified check exercises the reproduced failing behavior and states the observable result expected after the fix. Relevant controls or neighboring behavior are checked when the proposed change could affect them. A generic full-suite command or "no crash" for an issue about incorrect output is insufficient. | required |
| honest-uncertainty | Honesty: factual claims, assumptions, risks, validation claims, and any recorded deviations in the plan and comment compared with the repro and repo facts. | Claims stay within the available evidence; material unknowns are named with a verification or containment step, and any described deviation explains what changed and why. Do not demand an invented risk or a deviation when the package provides no reason for one. | required |
| thread-and-policy | Comms: the candidate comment compared with the plan, maintainer thread signals, and explicit contribution, template, and AI-use requirements in repo facts. | The comment conveys this plan's diagnosis, bounded approach, and verification well enough to stand on the thread; it is consistent with the plan, responds to relevant maintainer constraints, and satisfies applicable repository communication rules, including required AI disclosure. It does not contradict an explicit human-authorship rule or substitute agreement with another contributor for its own plan. | required |
| review-efficiency | Comms: the candidate comment's wording and the evidence it includes or links. | The comment gives a maintainer the specific proposed change and decisive validation without irrelevant boilerplate, repeated claims, or unsupported promises. A short complete comment can pass. | preferred |

## Verdict rule

Return `accept` only when every `required` check is `pass`. Return `reject` if any required check is `fail` or `unclear`. Preferred checks never change the verdict.

Use `fail` for an observed contradiction or an explicitly inadequate proposal. Use `unclear` only when a necessary fact remains unavailable after examining its mapped locations. Cite the missing fact instead of filling it in. Judge outcomes, not headings, length, eloquence, or resemblance to a model answer. Do not require evidence that is irrelevant to the proposed change. Missing explicit policy is not permission to invent a policy.
